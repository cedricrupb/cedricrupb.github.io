I am co-organizing the
[Clarification Challenge](https://serval-uni-lu.github.io/clarification-competition/),
which investigates how AI coding assistants can recognize
ambiguous requirements and ask useful clarifying questions
before generating code.

We invite researchers and developers interested in
trustworthy AI-assisted software engineering to participate.

[Visit the challenge website](https://serval-uni-lu.github.io/clarification-competition/).

I have joined Humboldt-Universität zu Berlin as a
DFG Walter Benjamin Fellow.

My project investigates how LLM-based agents can use
software verification tools and hypotheses about
intended program behavior to detect and repair software bugs.

I look forward to developing this research direction
at the intersection of software engineering, formal
verification, and machine learning.
